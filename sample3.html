About Us

College of Engineering Chengannur (CEC) was established by the government of kerala under the IHRD.The College is approved by AICTE and affiliated by APJ Abdul Kalam technological University (KTU).A premier government-aided engineering institute located in chengannur.It is highly regarded for its academic performance,high placement rates,and innovative student culture.
